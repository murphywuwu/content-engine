# recommendations/

First-class **third-party product and tool library**. This is separate from
`products/`: entries here are products the profile may review, compare, or
recommend, not products EasySociable owns or sells.

```text
Profile + Needs + review evidence
  → Topic
  → optional recommendation_ids
  → Run
```

## Rules

- Do not treat a recommendation as an owned Product or as a product CTA.
- Do not copy vendor marketing claims into `quote` or evidence.
- Record the research date and source URLs. Prices and features can change.
- Record affiliate, sponsorship, or other commercial relationships when known.
- A recommendation needs a review basis: observed workflow, named source,
  comparison, or user-provided evidence. An empty entry is not a review.
